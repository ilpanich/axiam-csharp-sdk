No profile means the segment is not a placement candidate, so the delete removes every device placed on it: the write gate asks network:profiles_write (H1) and the placing right, network:grant on the segment (and on /network/quarantine/<site> for a quarantine segment), and refuses a caller that may join the segment (H6).

DeleteSegmentAsync(Guid, long, CancellationToken)

The segment, its reply profile and its resource node, in one transaction. Role assignments and grants naming the node are not touched; the node is simply gone. A segment that has a profile takes the devices placed on it along, so the write gate asks the placing right too (network:grant on the segment, and on /network/quarantine/<site> for a quarantine segment).

A replacement of the tenant's Session-Timeout cap, conditional on version. Needs network:segments_write at the /network root (the cap is the envelope every profile is clamped to); network:profiles_write alone does not suffice, nor does a grant at a site below the root.
