Remarks

This is the string a browser passes to PublicKeyCredential.parseCreationOptionsFromJSON(), and the value a MAUI or Uno app hands to the platform's WebAuthn bridge. It is the inner options object: the publicKey wrapper belongs to the DOM's CredentialCreationOptions, and the platform JSON APIs do not want it.

Pure local computation, no I/O, hence a property rather than an Async method. Nothing is defaulted, dropped or reordered on the way through (§24.0).
